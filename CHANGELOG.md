## [1.0.0]

### Added

- OPDS 1.x Atom catalog discovery and parsing.
- OpenSearch discovery and paginated search.
- Read-only catalog browsing with navigation, related catalogs, and pagination.
- A local download queue with manual and scheduled processing.
- EPUB and FB2 acquisition downloads.
- Structural validation for EPUB, plain FB2, and FB2 inside ZIP archives.
- Local persistence for configuration, queue state, jobs, notifications, and run history.
- Windows desktop packaging in onedir and onefile forms.
